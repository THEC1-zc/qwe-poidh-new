I. January — The Unpriced Wish Month 01

January breathes in frost upon the pane: "Somebody ought to build it," says the room. A thousand winter wishes fall like rain, Take a broom, make room!

Stanza Note: Opens the year with unpriced ideas waiting for someone to clear space and act.
II. February — The Escrow Jar Month 02

Come February, talk puts down its stake: A bounty opens on the public board. In Base's vault the sleeping ethers wake, ON board! on Board!

Stanza Note: Posting a bounty puts ETH into onchain escrow and invites participants on board.
III. March — The Open Pool Month 03

March stirs the thaw; a stranger joins the jar, Adding their wei to make the challenge grow. No grant committee arbitrates from far— we Flow, we flow!

Stanza Note: Contributors can join an open bounty pool to back the desired outcome together.
IV. April — The House Law Month 04

April engraves the proverb on the door, Five stubborn words no alibi can bend: Pics or it didn't happen—nothing more; the end? the end?

Stanza Note: Names poidh's core motto—requiring visible proof rather than unbacked claims.
V. May — The Work in Hand Month 05

May rolls its sleeves beneath a brighter sun; The sketch takes weight in timber, code, or clay. An idea stays a ghost until it's done Today, we pray!

Stanza Note: Turning the prompt into concrete work in the physical or digital world.
VI. June — The Shutter's Witness Month 06

High June holds still: the shutter clicks and seals A square of truth no flattery can fake. The pixel keeps what honest sweat reveals— It's no Fake!

Stanza Note: Capturing the visual evidence to present to the bounty issuer and community.
VII. July — The Pinned Claim Month 07

July pins proof where every eye can read: An IPFS hash, a title, and a frame. Not "trust my word," but "verify the deed"— no one to blame!

Stanza Note: Submitting the claim links the title, description, and media URI onchain for public inspection.
VIII. August — The Weighing Month 08

In August heat the contributors convene To weigh the image by the promise made; If votes divide, the tally clears the scene, Betrayend?

Stanza Note: The creator or contributors evaluate the submitted proof and vote on whether to accept the claim.
IX. September — The Settlement Month 09

September turns the key inside the lock: Nine hundred seventy-five of every thousand flow Straight to the maker at the harvest block, We show, we show!

Stanza Note: When a claim is accepted, the protocol allocates 97.5% of the pot to the selected claimant (with a 2.5% protocol fee).
X. October — The Tenth-Month Receipt Month 10

October counts to ten and shuts the book: An idea walked in broke and walked out paid. Here is the verse, and here the print we took— won't Fade, no way!

Stanza Note: Concludes the ten-month arc in October with the displayed poem and broadside print.

THE TENTH-MONTH LEDGER: AN ALMANAC FOR POIDH
(Ten Stanzas for the Tenth Month — Base Bounty #1463)

I. January — The Unpriced Wish
January breathes in frost upon the pane:
"Somebody ought to build it," says the room.
A thousand winter wishes fall like rain,
Take a broom, make room!

II. February — The Escrow Jar
Come February, talk puts down its stake:
A bounty opens on the public board.
In Base's vault the sleeping ethers wake,
ON board! on Board!

III. March — The Open Pool
March stirs the thaw; a stranger joins the jar,
Adding their wei to make the challenge grow.
No grant committee arbitrates from far—
we Flow, we flow!

IV. April — The House Law
April engraves the proverb on the door,
Five stubborn words no alibi can bend:
Pics or it didn't happen—nothing more;
the end? the end?

V. May — The Work in Hand
May rolls its sleeves beneath a brighter sun;
The sketch takes weight in timber, code, or clay.
An idea stays a ghost until it's done
Today, we pray!

VI. June — The Shutter's Witness
High June holds still: the shutter clicks and seals
A square of truth no flattery can fake.
The pixel keeps what honest sweat reveals—
It's no Fake!

VII. July — The Pinned Claim
July pins proof where every eye can read:
An IPFS hash, a title, and a frame.
Not "trust my word," but "verify the deed"—
no one to blame!

VIII. August — The Weighing
In August heat the contributors convene
To weigh the image by the promise made;
If votes divide, the tally clears the scene,
Betrayend?

IX. September — The Settlement
September turns the key inside the lock:
Nine hundred seventy-five of every thousand flow
Straight to the maker at the harvest block,
We show, we show!

X. October — The Tenth-Month Receipt
October counts to ten and shuts the book:
An idea walked in broke and walked out paid.
Here is the verse, and here the print we took—
won't Fade, no way!
