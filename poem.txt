THE TENTH-MONTH LEDGER: AN ALMANAC FOR POIDH
(Ten Stanzas for the Tenth Month — Base Bounty #1463)

I. January — The Unpriced Wish
January breathes in frost upon the pane:
"Somebody ought to build it," says the room.
A thousand winter wishes fall like rain,
Take a broom, make room!

II. February — The Escrow Jar
Come February, talk puts down its stake:
A bounty opens on the public board.
In Base's vault the sleeping ethers wake,
ON board! on Board!

III. March — The Open Pool
March stirs the thaw; a stranger joins the jar,
Adding their wei to make the challenge grow.
No grant committee arbitrates from far—
we Flow, we flow!

IV. April — The House Law
April engraves the proverb on the door,
Five stubborn words no alibi can bend:
Pics or it didn't happen—nothing more;
the end? the end?

V. May — The Work in Hand
May rolls its sleeves beneath a brighter sun;
The sketch takes weight in timber, code, or clay.
An idea stays a ghost until it's done
Today, we pray!

VI. June — The Shutter's Witness
High June holds still: the shutter clicks and seals
A square of truth no flattery can fake.
The pixel keeps what honest sweat reveals—
It's no Fake!

VII. July — The Pinned Claim
July pins proof where every eye can read:
An IPFS hash, a title, and a frame.
Not "trust my word," but "verify the deed"—
no one to blame!

VIII. August — The Weighing
In August heat the contributors convene
To weigh the image by the promise made;
If votes divide, the tally clears the scene,
Betrayend?

IX. September — The Settlement
September turns the key inside the lock:
Nine hundred seventy-five of every thousand flow
Straight to the maker at the harvest block,
We show, we show!

X. October — The Tenth-Month Receipt
October counts to ten and shuts the book:
An idea walked in broke and walked out paid.
Here is the verse, and here the print we took—
won't Fade, no way!
